## First Comment (Founder Story)
Hey everyone —

I run a few SaaS products on Stripe and started noticing something weird in my transaction logs. Payment intents tagged with weird agent strings. ChatGPT. Perplexity. Claude. AI agents were browsing my site and buying stuff.

I had no idea how much revenue was coming from agents vs humans, or which agents converted best. So I built AgentViz.

It's a dead-simple dashboard that tells you:
- How much revenue each AI agent is generating
- Conversion rates — agent shoppers vs traditional
- Agent commerce trends over time

The market for agentic commerce is projected to go from $7.7B to $65B by 2033. The agents are already here — are you tracking them?

Connect your Stripe account (direct API key, no OAuth flow), and you're live in 60 seconds. Three plans: $29/$79/$199 depending on volume. Free tier for up to 1,000 agent transactions/month.

Completely self-serve. No onboarding calls. No sales demos. Just connect and see the data.

Would love feedback and happy to answer questions!

— Scy
